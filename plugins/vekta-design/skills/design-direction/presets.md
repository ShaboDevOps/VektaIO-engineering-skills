## Picking hues so products don't look alike
- Keep a list of each product's primary hue (degrees) and archetype in its `design.md` → "Differs from our other products by".
- New product's primary hue should be ≥ 30° away from every sibling's, or use a clearly different saturation/lightness character.
- Vary at least four of: hue, heading font, radius, depth model, motion character, icon set, signature element.

## Icon sets (pick one per product)
Lucide (outline, neutral) · Phosphor (outline/duotone/fill — expressive) · Tabler (outline, technical) · Material Symbols (variable weight/fill — native feel on Android) · custom set for strong brands.
